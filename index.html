3 Look

Each drawing goes outline first, then shading, then colour, and finishes a little before its time is up. Pick several characters to draw them one after another (numbers show the order). The green / orange / red dot rates how well each image sketches, and the speed line tells you whether the pencil will visibly move. Uploads are remembered in this browser.

🖼️ What images work best?
  • One character, full body, centred, filling most of the image.
  • Plain or white background. A transparent PNG is best; busy backgrounds turn into messy lines.
  • Cartoon style with clear dark outlines and flat colours sketches most cleanly. Line art and comic style work great too.
  • Good contrast: not too dark, blurry or low-light. At least ~800 px on the long side.
  • Avoid text and logos, tiny details everywhere, real photos of crowds or rooms, and screenshots.
  • For long videos (2+ hours), use several drawings or Detailed, so the pencil keeps moving at a visible speed.
  • Your own characters only: drawn by you or made with an image generator. Characters from games, anime or cartoons can get copyright claims.
  • Image-generator prompt that works well:cute cartoon [your character, e.g. a fox astronaut], full body, standing, thick clean black outlines, flat colours, simple shapes, plain white background, children's book style
